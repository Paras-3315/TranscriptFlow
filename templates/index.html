FAQ

Is this tool free?
Yes! Extracting existing captions from YouTube videos is completely free. AI transcription for videos without captions uses one credit per video.
What languages are supported?
Caption extraction supports any language available on YouTube. AI transcription auto-detects the spoken language and produces a transcript.
Why can't I get a transcript for some videos?
Some videos have captions disabled by the creator, or are private/age-restricted. In those cases, use our AI Transcription option.
How does AI Summarization work?
Once your transcript is extracted, click "Generate AI Summary." Our AI reads the full transcript and returns a concise, accurate summary in your chosen format.
Can I download the transcript?
Yes! Click the "Download .txt" button to save the full transcript as a plain text file to your device.
