Our Story

TranscriptFlow started as a personal project — a simple script to pull captions from YouTube videos for note-taking and research. After realizing how useful it was for saving time, improving accessibility, and making content searchable, we decided to build it into a proper tool anyone could use.

Today, TranscriptFlow lets you pull free captions in seconds, use AI to transcribe any video without subtitles, and summarize even hour-long videos into concise notes — all for free, right in your browser.

What We Stand For

🔓 Free Access

Core transcript extraction is and always will be free. We believe information access shouldn't have a price tag.

♿ Accessibility

Transcripts make video content usable for people who are deaf, hard of hearing, or simply prefer reading.

🔒 Privacy First

We don't store your transcripts or URLs. Your content stays yours — we're just the pipe.
