Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2025

Overview

TranscriptFlow ("we", "us", or "our") operates the TranscriptFlow website and service. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our free YouTube transcript extraction and AI summarization tool.

By using TranscriptFlow, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

We collect minimal data necessary to provide our service:

  • YouTube URLs — You submit URLs to extract transcripts. We process these to fetch captions or audio, but do not permanently store them.
  • Usage Data — Anonymous analytics such as page views, browser type, and general geographic region, collected via standard web analytics tools (e.g. Google Analytics).
  • Contact Information — If you reach out via our contact form, we collect your name and email address solely to respond to your inquiry.
  • Cookies — We use cookies to serve relevant advertising through Google AdSense. You may opt out via your browser settings.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and improve the TranscriptFlow service.
  • To respond to your support or contact requests.
  • To serve relevant ads via Google AdSense (subject to Google's own privacy policy).
  • To monitor usage patterns and fix technical issues.

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

Third-Party Services

TranscriptFlow integrates with the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdSense — Advertising. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Deepgram — AI audio transcription for videos without captions.
  • Google Gemini — AI summarization of transcripts.
  • YouTube Transcript API — Fetching publicly available captions from YouTube.

When you use AI Transcription, your video's audio is temporarily processed by Deepgram and is not retained by us after the transcript is returned.

Data Retention

We do not store transcripts, summaries, or YouTube URLs on our servers beyond the duration of your active session. Contact form submissions are retained only as long as needed to address your inquiry.

Children's Privacy

TranscriptFlow is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights including access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy
